# Cover photo credits

These bundled Wikimedia Commons thumbnails were visually reviewed on 2026-09-23. No runtime image search is used. Images are resized by Wikimedia, with no local cropping or recoloring; the app displays the entire image. Each image retains its individual license below; the app source license does not replace it.

To add a destination: select a landscape photograph, verify its Commons file page and license, download a bounded thumbnail, inspect it at card size, add exact aliases to `lib/models/destination_cover.dart`, and update credits and tests. Do not use city-name substring matching or images of logos, maps, collages, or unrelated places.
